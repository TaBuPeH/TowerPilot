1 · Find your emulator pending

Scans MuMu, BlueStacks, LDPlayer and PATH adb installs and asks each daemon for connected instances. Whatever answers here can be driven.

2 · Verify the resolution pending

Every template and coordinate assumes a 1080×2560 portrait display. Pick a device above, then check.

3 · Point the config at it pending

Press use for config on the device row in step 1 - the wizard writes adb.exe and the active instance's serial into config.yaml (timestamped backup, as every save here) and runs adb connect. Nothing to copy by hand. Editing those two fields in Configuration still works for odd setups. The live-screen panel on Status confirms the wiring.

5 · Scan the account's capabilities

Once the wiring works: Profile tab → Start scan. It reads the account's card presets, guardian chips and modules into a draft profile; the battle phase (opt-in) adds Ultimate Weapons and the ability row. Unknown items land in the harvest queue above - template them, rescan, confirm.

4 · Harvest account-specific templates

Buttons and HUD art ship with the engine, but module icons, guardian chips and card art depend on YOUR account (rarity changes the art). Run the game, watch the Templates tab's "missing" list, and crop each flagged item from a native screenshot. Match rarity: build modules are matched at their equipped rarity, spares at theirs.
